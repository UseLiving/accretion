      What it is

      Accretion is an arena for communities. A community launches its own coin and becomes a clan. Every day all clans in orbit compete on the same task: most buyers, biggest inflow, fewest sells. The three best clans of the day get their coin bought back and burned by the Core, the arena's shared pool, and the clan owner earns a share of every trade.

      01

      Launch a clan: name, ticker, emoji, 0.1 SOL gas tank.

      02

      Gather 20 holders with $5+ each to enter orbit.

      03

      Compete in the day's contest until 00:00 UTC.

      04

      Top 3 get bought back and burned by the Core.

      How it runs

      • A clan is a coin paired with $ACCRETE and launched through Accretion.
      • Hold a clan's coin and you count for it. Sell it and you don't.
      • A clan enters orbit once 20 wallets each hold at least $5 of its coin.
      • The Core is filled only by trading fees. It never sends anyone money.

      Contests

      One contest a day, finishing at 00:00 UTC. The standings at the finish are what count. Only real buyers count for buyer contests: a wallet has to buy at least $20 in a swap and still hold it at the finish.

      At the finish the Core buys the top 3 coins on the open market and burns them, 50 / 30 / 20% of what it holds, capped at 5% of the money in each coin, in small chunks at random times over the next 24 hours. With fewer than 3 clans in orbit there is no contest and the pot rolls to tomorrow.

      Where the money goes

      Every trade of a clan coin carries a 2% fee. Slide to see where it goes:

      Volume traded: $1,000
      The Core · 1.2%$12
      Clan owner · 0.6%$6
      Burn $ACCRETE · 0.2%$2

      Nobody tops up the Core. All of it comes from trading fees. It keeps at most three days of inflow; the rest is burned as $ACCRETE.

      Ladder and beefs

      • The Ladder: once per clan per milestone ($250K, $500K, $1M market cap) the Core burns a share into that coin.
      • Beefs: a clan owner challenges another for 24 hours. Each puts up their own stake, and both stakes are bought and burned into the winner's coin.

      Why $ACCRETE

      Every clan coin is paired with $ACCRETE, not SOL. Buying any clan routes SOL → $ACCRETE → clan coin in one swap. The Core holds $ACCRETE, and every clan trade burns a little of it.
