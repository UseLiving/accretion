# Accretion ($ACCRETE)

Clans orbit the Core and compete in one contest a day. At midnight UTC the Core buys back and burns the top three.

**Live site:** https://useliving.github.io/accretion/  
**X:** https://x.com/UseAccretion

## What you can do
- Rotate and zoom the arena: a black hole Core with clans orbiting as planets and trades flying in as comets
- Buy, sell and burn clan coins with a play wallet, and watch your trades move the leaderboard
- Launch your own clan and watch it gather 20 holders to enter orbit
- Start a 24-hour beef against another clan
- Browse the daily contest schedule and set reminders
- Ambient space soundtrack (toggle in the top bar)

Single self-contained `index.html`. Progress is saved in your browser.

> Demo: trades are simulated and the wallet uses play money. Not an investment and not financial advice.
